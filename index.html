Frequently asked questions

How do I pay for my order?

At checkout, choose cash on delivery, card or mobile wallet.

How do I use a voucher?

Enter your voucher code in the checkout form. Only one valid voucher can be used per order.

How can I see where my rider is?

The order tracking screen shows your order status and the rider's live location.

What if the restaurant rejects my order?

The order is cancelled, and if you already paid, you are refunded.

Can I repeat an earlier order?

Open your order history and tap reorder. The items go back into your cart, as long as the restaurant and dishes are still available.
